🛒 Shop Smarter on Amazon

Amazon Prime Autoset automatically filters for Prime-eligible products and sorts by price (low to high) on every Amazon search. Find the best deals instantly!

✨ Key Features:
• Auto Prime Filter - Shows only Prime-eligible items
• Price Sorting - Lowest to highest automatically
• Global Support - Works on all Amazon marketplaces
• Privacy First - No data collection, local storage only
• Dark Mode - Adapts to your browser theme

🌍 Supported Marketplaces:
North America: amazon.com, amazon.ca, amazon.com.mx
Europe: amazon.de, amazon.co.uk, amazon.fr, amazon.it, amazon.es, amazon.nl, amazon.se, amazon.pl
Asia Pacific: amazon.co.jp, amazon.com.au, amazon.in, amazon.sg
Other: amazon.com.br, amazon.ae, amazon.eg, amazon.tr

🔒 Privacy Commitment:
• No data collection
• No tracking or analytics
• No external servers
• All settings stored locally
